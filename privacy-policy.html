Bwriter Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

Bwriter is a book-writing app for Android. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is stored, and the choices you have. By using Bwriter, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address. These identify your books, your in-app mail, and your community access.
  • Your content. The books, chapters, comments, and character notes you write. You choose whether and when to sync a book to the cloud.
  • Advertising data. Bwriter shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect your device's advertising ID and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Service data. Google Firebase, which operates the Bwriter back room, processes technical data needed to run sign-in, storage, and the community registry.

Where your books are stored

  • On your device, in the app's local database. This is where you write.
  • In your own Google Drive, when you sync: a copy of the complete book is saved in a folder named "Bwriter". The app can only see files it created; it cannot read the rest of your Drive.
  • In the Bwriter back room (Google Firebase), when you sync: a copy of the complete book is stored under your account so the app can restore your library when you reinstall or change devices.
  • In the community registry, when you sync a book as Public: the book's full text is stored with its registry entry and can be read by any signed-in Bwriter user. Books synced as Private are not shown in the community catalog, and their text can be read only by you and the Editor-in-Chief.

In-App Credit & Author Keys

  • The app generates a cryptographic keypair on your device; the private key leaves the device only inside the passphrase-sealed backup you choose to store in your own Google Drive.
  • The back room stores issuance and claim records — your author address (an identifier derived from your public key), credit amounts, the book's registry id, and timestamps. Issuance records are digitally signed by the Editor in Chief; claim records are bound to their issuance — the amount and the book's registry id are copied from it — and carry the proof of work that claimed them. These records cannot be altered after the fact.
  • Your passphrase is never collected, transmitted, or stored.

How information is used

  • To save, sync, and restore your books.
  • To operate the community registry, in-app mail, and co-writing features.
  • To show ads and to provide the optional 24-hour ad-free period after a rewarded ad. The ad-free period is stored only on your device.
  • To protect the community, including suspending accounts that abuse the service.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not claim any ownership or licensing rights in your manuscripts.

Sharing

  • Google provides sign-in, Drive storage, Firebase, and AdMob, and processes data under its own privacy policy.
  • Other users can read only the books you choose to sync as Public.
  • Legal requirements. We may disclose information if required by law.

Your choices and deletion

  • Sync books as Private to keep them out of the community catalog.
  • Delete books from your device at any time.
  • Remove the app's files from your Google Drive at any time, in the "Bwriter" folder.
  • To request deletion of your account and the copies stored in the Bwriter back room, contact us at the address below. We will delete your cloud copies and registry entries and confirm by email.

Data retention

Cloud copies are kept until you delete them or ask us to delete them. Books removed from the registry are removed from the back room as part of that deletion.

Children

Bwriter is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date, and the agreement shown in the app will be updated to match.
