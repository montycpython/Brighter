# Bwriter

**Write a book to completion.**

Bwriter is an Android book-writing app built on a simple trade: you bring the idea, the app brings the craft. The typography, the book structure, and the publishing groundwork are carried for you, so a finished manuscript comes out the other side — ready to become a physical book.

## What it does

- **Write in real book structure** — chapters and sections organized as front matter, body, and back matter, with recto and verso leaves, Roman and Arabic page numbering, and standard trim sizes.
- **Chicago style built in** — style checks in the editor, drawn from the Chicago Manual of Style: punctuation, block quotes, hyphenation, and more.
- **Print-grade PDF export** — a finished manuscript, laid out as a book, with a generated table of contents.
- **Your book lives in your own Google Drive** — every manuscript syncs to a Bwriter folder in your Drive. Your words stay yours: 100% copyright, no royalties, no lock-in.
- **A human-gated registry** — finished books are submitted to the studio registry, where each one is read and approved by a person before it is published to the library.
- **Proof of authorship** — every book carries a signed stamp chain and a BISN, a birth-derived identifier generated from the book itself, so authorship can be proven without borrowing an ISBN.
- **Editors and collaborators** — invite co-writers, assign an editor to your book, and let the app handle the credit in the front and back matter automatically.
- **In-app credit** — an approved book earns in-app credit for its author and its editors. Credit can be sent to other authors inside the app. It is not money and has no cash value.

## The studio

The registry is run by the Editor in Chief. Books enter it only by approval. AI assistance exists in the app, but it is optional and never the point — Bwriter is valuable with or without it.

Tap the golden emblem in the app to read **The Origin of Writing** — where the written word came from, and why it still carries weight.

- `./gradlew bundleRelease` for the release bundle
